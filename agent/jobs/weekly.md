Read the notes under `knowledge/notes/` and write a summary of the week to
`work/reports/weekly.md`.

Group by theme, not by date. Say what changed and what is still open. If the notes do not
support a conclusion, say so rather than filling the gap — a short honest report is worth more
than a long confident one.
